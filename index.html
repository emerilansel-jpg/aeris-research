How We Work

A Rigorous Four-Stage Process

Every Aeris research deliverable passes through four structured stages — ensuring our ratings reflect reality, not vendor marketing.

01

Structured Data Collection

We gather product data through structured hands-on evaluations, API testing, vendor briefings, and end-user interviews across each coverage category.

02

Proprietary Scoring Framework

Our analysts score each vendor across 40+ dimensions — from technical capability and roadmap credibility to pricing transparency and support quality.

03

Expert Review & Calibration

Every report undergoes structured peer review by a senior analyst and calibration against our longitudinal vendor performance database before publication.

04

Decision-Ready Delivery

Intelligence is distilled into concise, action-oriented formats — from rapid vendor snapshots to full strategic reports with scenario modelling.
