How We Work

Our Methodology

A four-stage research process engineered to deliver accurate, actionable, and completely vendor-neutral technology intelligence.

01. Structured Data Collection

Our analysts gather product data through structured hands-on evaluations, API testing, vendor briefings, end-user interviews, and enterprise practitioner panels. We never rely solely on vendor-provided information — all claims are independently tested.

02. Proprietary Scoring Framework

Every vendor and product is scored across 40+ dimensions spanning technical capability, roadmap credibility, pricing transparency, support quality, security posture, and enterprise readiness. All dimension weights are documented and available to clients.

03. Expert Review & Calibration

Every report undergoes structured peer review by a senior analyst and calibration against our longitudinal vendor performance database. Historical accuracy of assessments is tracked and published annually.

04. Decision-Ready Delivery

Intelligence is distilled into concise, action-oriented formats — from rapid vendor snapshots (2-4 pages) to full strategic reports with scenario modelling (50-90 pages). All formats are structured for executive consumption, not analyst gratification.

Independence Policy

Aeris Research does not accept vendor sponsorship, paid placement, or consulting fees from companies it covers in research. Analysts are prohibited from holding financial positions in covered companies. Our complete independence policy is available on request.
