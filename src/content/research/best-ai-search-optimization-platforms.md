## Cross‑Vendor Findings & Patterns

Aeris Research identified five clear patterns across the AI search market:

### 1. The Death of Position-One Dominance

Top rankings no longer guarantee search traffic. Data from Ahrefs (February 2026) shows top organic pages lose **58% of their clicks** when an AI Overview appears. Position-one click rates dropped from 0.03734 to 0.01568 in comparative studies.

### 2. Citations Are the New Search Currency

While overall search clicks are down, cited brands win big. Seer Interactive (November 2025) reported that brands cited inside AI Overviews earn **35% more organic clicks** than non-cited sites.

### 3. Paid Search Is Heavily Impacted

AI search impacts paid ads even more than organic links. Seer Interactive (November 2025) recorded a **68% decline** in paid ad CTR on queries where AI answers appear.

### 4. Branded Search Protects Traffic

Branded queries remain strong in generative search environments. Industry data published by Neil Patel (June 2026) showed an **18.7% CTR increase** for branded queries when the AI summary cites the brand.

### 5. Shift Beyond Search Engines

Traffic behavior is changing across the whole internet. Seer Interactive (November 2025) found that organic CTRs dropped **41% year-over-year** even on queries without a visible AI Overview. Users are shifting directly to AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude to answer questions.
