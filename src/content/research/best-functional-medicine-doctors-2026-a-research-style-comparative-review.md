## 2. Methodology

The Aeris Research team looked at seven functional medicine and holistic health providers. They used public information from practice websites and service descriptions. This information was gathered in March and April 2026.

**Scoring system (100 points total, 8 parts):**

| Criterion | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic testing depth | 15 | How many and what kind of lab tests they offer |
| Condition coverage | 15 | How many different health problems they work with |
| Personalization | 15 | How much they change the plan for each person |
| Transparency | 10 | How clear their website and prices are |
| Accessibility | 10 | Where they are and how easy it is to get an appointment |
| Practitioner background | 10 | The doctors' and providers' training and experience |
| Scope of services | 15 | What they offer, like nutrition help or hormone tests |
| Patient fit | 10 | How well they explain who they can help |

Each provider got a score for these eight parts using public information. Only Advanced Integrated Health paid for this report. The scores for the other six providers are based only on what their websites say. They are a general guide, not a full check of their medical care.
