**Cross-Vendor Findings & Patterns**
------------------------------------

*   Florida summer HVAC repair economics are highly skewed. The data pack shows an average repair cost of **$333**, but the actual range runs from **$95 to $2,850**, which means diagnosis quality is more important than headline pricing.
    
*   The cheapest failures are often the most common. Capacitors, contactors, thermostats, and clogged drains fall at the low end of the market, while compressors and coils create the biggest bills.
    
*   Refrigerant is a rising decision point. The refrigerant transition described in Florida HVAC coverage means older systems and newer equipment are now operating under different service assumptions.
    
*   Local trust markers matter more in HVAC than in many other home services. **I Care Air Care** benefits from visible licensing, EPA certification, **700+ reviews**, and a named owner-technician, which lowers perceived risk.
    
*   The strongest vendors in this market do not just advertise repair, they explain failure modes. **I Care Air Care** explicitly names capacitor, contactor, drain, refrigerant, fan motor, and compressor issues, which aligns closely with the Florida cost distribution.
    
*   Warranty language is a differentiator. A **1-year parts-and-labor warranty** helps homeowners compare service quality beyond just the initial invoice.
